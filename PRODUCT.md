# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Idea owners:** students with a project idea who cannot find skilled teammates beyond their own friends.
- **Builders:** students with skills, often juniors, who want real projects to work on and proof of what they built.
- **Coordinators (admins):** a faculty coordinator or club lead who verifies that each account belongs to a real student of the campus.

One student account can be an owner, a builder, or both, and switch at any time.

## Product Purpose

Sandbox is a campus-only platform that connects students who have a project idea with verified students whose skills are proven by their real GitHub work. Success is real student teams formed and projects built. It launches at LPU and is built so more colleges can join later.

## Positioning

Only verified students of the campus can post or join, and skills come from a student's own original repositories (read through GitHub's sign-in), not typed claims. It is for ongoing campus projects, not one-off competitions.

## Operating Context

Students sign in with Google and can browse straight away. They verify once, the first time they post an idea or ask to join: UMS single sign-on once the university approves it, or an ID card photo a coordinator reviews (the photo is deleted after the decision). Owners see builders ranked by proven skills, with a proof line for each. Only a short summary of an idea is public; the full plan is visible to accepted teammates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built today: landing, Google sign-in, dashboard, ID-card verification, coordinator queue (approve / reject with reason).
- Next: GitHub skill profiles, posting ideas, ranked matching, invites and applications, team formation.
- Registration numbers are never shown to other students; one registration number backs one account.
- Never collect UMS passwords; UMS login happens on the university's own page.
- Always free for students: verification, posting ideas and matching. No data selling, no ads.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Sandbox. Line: "Find teammates by what they've built, not what they claim."
- Palette pinned by the founder: Navy #14213D, Cream #F4EDE0, Coral #FF6B5B.
- The public site is bold (navy with coral glow); the signed-in app is calm and readable (cream ground, navy text, coral for actions).

## Evidence on Hand

No real users, testimonials, partner logos or usage numbers yet. Example profiles and matches on the site are illustrative and labelled so. Never invent student counts, quotes or university endorsements.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over claims: every skill shown carries where it came from.
2. Privacy by default: private data stays private, and the product says what it keeps and deletes.
3. Verify once, at the moment it matters, never before browsing.
4. Every rule that protects students is enforced by the database, not only the page.
